A product point of view

Brad’s Product Paradigms

Ten beliefs shape how I run product. Four of them come up in every serious conversation.

Paradigm 1

Direction beats speed

Speed is table stakes now. The question is whether we’re fast at the right things.

Paradigm 3

Strategy is what we won’t do

Show me what you decided not to do, and I’ll tell you whether you have a strategy.

Paradigm 6

Demos aren’t products

A demo proves the idea can work. Production proves customers can trust it.

Paradigm 9

Builder-executive

I lead through others, but I never stopped building. Ask me what I made last month.

All ten, with the belief and the proof

I-D3-M operating model

Nothing reaches engineering without clearing two gates.

  1. Intake
  2. Discovery
  3. Invest?
  4. Definition
  5. Build?
  6. Development
  7. Measure

Briefs, not PRDs

Initiative briefs, opportunity statements, prototypes and technical guardrails replace long requirement documents.

Scored, not lobbied

Every bet is scored on Impact, Effort and Confidence and tied to an outcome pillar on the roadmap.

Reviewed every quarter

A quarterly strategy review restates the bets, checks results and keeps a killed list so what we dropped stays dropped.

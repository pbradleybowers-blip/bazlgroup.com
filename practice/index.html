      Your privacy

      Plain answers about what happens to your information.

      This is a practice space, and you should feel comfortable using it. Here is exactly what happens to what you share.

      Your resume and the job description

      They are sent to Anthropic's Claude, the AI that prepares and runs your interview. This site does not save them. They pass through this site's small backend on Cloudflare only on their way to Claude. If you give a job link, the backend opens that page to read the posting.

      Your answers and the transcript

      They stay in your browser while you practice. To write each reply, the feedback and the debrief, the conversation so far is sent to Claude, and this site does not save it. When you close or refresh the page, the transcript is gone.

      What is saved

      • A short summary of each finished session, saved under your access code so your history follows you to any device: the date, role, company, level, panelist, mode, length, verdict and the recurring pattern from the debrief. Nothing else.
      • How many interviews your code has started and finished, and the date it was last used. This keeps the daily limit working. Brad can see these counts, never what anyone said.
      • You can delete your history at any time from the History page. That removes your session summaries; the counts stay.

      Your voice

      Turning your speech into text is done by your browser, not this site. Chrome sends your voice to Google's speech service to turn it into text, and other browsers may use their own service. If you'd rather not, press the keyboard button in the interview room and type your answers instead. The interviewer's voice also comes from your browser, and some of Chrome's voices are made on Google's servers.

      How Anthropic handles it

      Anthropic's published policy says that, by default, it does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial products, including the API this site uses, to train its models. It says it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, and may keep them longer when needed to enforce its usage policy or comply with the law. Read it yourself: training and how long data is kept.

      Your access code

      Your code is how the site knows your history is yours, so keep it to yourself. This browser remembers it so you don't have to type it each time. On a shared computer, choose "Use a different code" to make this browser forget it, along with any history saved only in this browser.
